Real-time glass, in five terms.

Iridescence — Thin-film wave interference producing angle-dependent spectral shifts across micro-depth boundaries.

Index of refraction — The optical ratio determining how sharply rays bend upon entering the smoked glass volume.

Dispersion — The chromatic splitting of white light into spectral wavelengths across high-curvature facets.

Transmission — The unattenuated proportion of radiant flux passing through the medium without volumetric absorption.

Roughness — Micro-facet normal variance softening internal specular reflections into soft studio gradients.
